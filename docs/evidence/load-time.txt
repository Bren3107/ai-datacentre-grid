Load time of the deployed app (check 4.1: the deployed URL loads in under five seconds)

Measured 31 Oct 2026, 06:15 UTC (17:15 in Sydney), from a home connection in Sydney, with
scripts/measure_load.py on main 3f2413d. Re-run it on the submitted commit:
    python scripts/measure_load.py

Method. Cold runs use a new browser profile each time, so nothing is cached (the same as DevTools
with "Disable cache" ticked, which is how BUILD_PLAN section 6 step 4 and DEPLOY_CHECKLIST measure it).
DOMContentLoaded and Load are the page's own navigation timing, the two figures DevTools prints in
the Network status bar. "question" and "4 charts" are seconds until the question heading, then all
four charts, are drawn inside the app.

After a sleep. Earlier the same day (about 06:12 UTC) the app was asleep: the page showed
"Yes, get this app back up!", and after the click the four charts took 38.6 s. The keep-awake
workflow (merged 31 Oct, 06:09 UTC) visits every 3 hours so a marker should not meet that page.

Reading. Load, the figure DevTools reports and the one our plan measures, was 3.1 to 4.0 s with
an empty cache: under 5 s. Content takes longer: with an empty cache the question appeared after
6.4 to 7.1 s and all four charts after 9.4 to 10.5 s; on a repeat visit, 3.0 to 3.2 s and 4.5 to
4.8 s. Most of the empty-cache time is the browser downloading Streamlit's and Plotly's own code.
If a marker times visible content on a first visit, this check could fail.
